Varigram, a GB Studio font sheet.

Varigram is GB Cartographer's author's variable-width GB Studio font (2026-10-13), made from monogram by datagoblin
(https://datagoblin.itch.io/monogram, CC0 1.0 Universal; no attribution required, credited anyway): glyphs moved up to the cell's top,
descenders on g, j, p, q, y shortened to one row, and each glyph trimmed to its width with magenta. Characters 32-126.
Varigram is released under CC0 1.0 Universal too.
