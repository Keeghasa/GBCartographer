The fourth alpha of **GB Cartographer**, a pixel painter for [GB Studio](https://www.gbstudio.dev) projects.

> **Experimental alpha.** Only use it on GB Studio projects you have backed up (a copy, or version control): it
> writes into the project when you save. Close GB Studio while you work. Free during the alpha.

### What it does

- Open a GB Studio 4 project (4.2.0 – 4.3.2) and paint its backgrounds, sprite sheets, tilesets, fonts, emotes,
  avatars and UI pictures in their real palettes.
- Paint palettes straight onto tiles, put palettes in a scene's slots, manage the project's palettes.
- Tile tools: linked tiles, a seamless view, a priority brush, saved stamps (with auto-tiles), a text tool with your font sheets.
- See GB Studio's limits: the tile budget (with the tiles that repeat marked), screen edges, project health.
- Wizards: picture to background, dawn / sunset / night palettes, a palette set from Lospec or a picture, a new
  background, a tile budget fixer, a project check-up, and live cave / dungeon and overworld generators.
- The Map Room: lay backgrounds out as an adventure-style grid of screens and check that their edges meet.
- Graphics: credited palette sets (CC0 and public domain) and 63 placeholder graphics (CC0) to paint over.
- Backups of every file it overwrites, restorable from Backups.

### New in this alpha

- **Auto-tile stamps.** Mark a saved stamp as an auto-tile (the button beside its pencil) and it paints by rules:
  **9** box, **↔** / **↕** 3-slice, **P** path, **W** 47-tile Wang, **E** Wang edges, **C** corner Wang for two
  terrains, **F** wall faces, **▶** animated tiles. Each has a placeholder template in Graphics → Art → Auto-tiles
  to draw your own art over.
- **Generators.** The cave and dungeon are drawn in a new style (black for nothing, light floors, outlined walls two
  tiles tall), or with your own Wall face or Wang stamp; **Make map** cuts a generated map into screens and lays them
  out in the Map Room.
- **Time of day.** The Picture tab shows a scene at dawn, sunset or night (its palettes' D, S and N versions),
  fading between them; the wizard makes dawn, sunset and night versions (your palettes as painted are the day).
- **Closing safely.** Closing the app or a tab with unsaved changes lists what would change, with thumbnails: save to
  the GB Studio project, keep it in GB Cartographer (the app only), or discard.
- **Tidier project panel.** Hide pictures (they wait, greyed, at the bottom), duplicate one for a variant, or delete
  one (asked every time; its files go to the backups). Click a saved stamp to stamp with it. The empty part of the
  left rail and the gap beside the right panel hide and show those panels.
- **Fonts.** The text tool, font sample and dialogue preview read fonts exactly as GB Studio 4.3 does (fonts drawn in
  the Game Boy greens typed solid bars before; character maps in the font's .json work now).
- **Pictures from other projects** wear a plane on their tab and have their own window; unsaved names are orange and
  italic; the tab strip keeps the open picture in view and lists every open picture.
- Project health groups a palette's same-color notes and words low contrast plainly; the eraser makes stamps
  see-through.

### Opening it the first time (the builds aren't signed)

- **macOS:** open the `.dmg` and drag GB Cartographer to Applications. The first time you open it, macOS says it
  can't check the app for malware (checking needs a paid Apple developer account). Click **Done**, then go to System
  Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to GB Cartographer, then **Open Anyway**
  again. Or run this once in Terminal: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/GB Cartographer.app"`
- **Windows:** if SmartScreen says it protected your PC, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Linux:** make the AppImage executable (`chmod +x GB-Cartographer-*.AppImage`) and run it.

### Known limits

- No demo project in this build: its art joins once its artists have said yes. Open your own (backed-up) GB Studio
  project instead.
- Tested on macOS; the Windows and Linux builds are new. Please report problems in the issues.
