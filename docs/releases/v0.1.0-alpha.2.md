The second alpha of **GB Cartographer**, a pixel painter for [GB Studio](https://www.gbstudio.dev) projects.

> **Experimental alpha.** Only use it on GB Studio projects you have backed up (a copy, or version control): it
> writes into the project when you save. Close GB Studio while you work. Free during the alpha.

### New in this alpha

- **Art** in the Graphics window: 54 placeholder graphics (CC0) to add to a project and paint over in your own way:
  walking characters, things and animated things (chests, doors, levers, coins), emotes, avatars, window frames and
  cursors, tile sheets with tall oaks and pines, whole scenes (title, menu, town, house, shop, cave, overworld,
  platformer level, battle, dialogue, a point-and-click room), and a village cut into six screens for the Map Room.
  **Add** puts one in your project as a new picture (it never replaces a file); **Open** shows it as a new, unsaved picture.

Everything from 0.1.0-alpha.1 is still here: painting in GB Studio's palettes, the palette brush, tile tools, the
wizards and generators, the Map Room, palette sets, and backups of every file it overwrites.

### Opening it the first time (the builds aren't signed)

- **macOS:** open the `.dmg` and drag GB Cartographer to Applications. The first time, right-click (or
  Control-click) the app and choose **Open**, then **Open** again. If macOS still refuses, go to System Settings →
  Privacy & Security and click **Open Anyway** next to GB Cartographer.
- **Windows:** if SmartScreen says it protected your PC, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Linux:** make the AppImage executable (`chmod +x GB-Cartographer-*.AppImage`) and run it.

### Known limits

- No demo project in this build: its art joins once its artists have said yes. Open your own (backed-up) GB Studio
  project instead.
- Tested on macOS; the Windows and Linux builds are new. Please report problems in the issues.
