The first beta of **GB Cartographer**, a pixel painter and palette companion for [GB Studio](https://www.gbstudio.dev) projects.

> **Beta.** Use it on GB Studio projects you have backed up (a copy, or version control): it writes into the project
> when you save, and keeps a backup of every file it overwrites. Close GB Studio while you work.

### What it does

- Open a GB Studio 4 project (4.2.0 – 4.3.2) and paint its backgrounds, sprite sheets, tilesets, fonts, emotes,
  avatars and UI pictures in their real palettes, then save straight back into the project.
- Paint palettes onto tiles (the palette brush, or **Palette fill** for every copy of a tile), put palettes in a
  scene's slots, manage and rename the project's palettes.
- Paint a scene's **collisions** for every GB Studio scene type (and plugin scene types), and tile **priority**.
- Tile tools: linked tiles, a seamless view, saved stamps with auto-tiles (9-slice, Wang, roads, areas with edges,
  cliffs, forests, animated tiles), a text tool with your font sheets, solid and pattern fills.
- See GB Studio's limits: the tile budget, screen edges, a project check-up with a fix for each issue.
- **Wizards**, each on one page: picture to background, dawn / sunset / night palettes, a palette set, a tile budget
  fixer, the project check-up, live cave / dungeon and overworld generators, and **3D Snapshot**: turn a 3D model
  (.glb, .gltf, .fbx, .obj and more), light it, and take its picture in the four shades as a background or sprite.
- The **Map Room**: backgrounds laid out as a grid of screens, their edges checked.
- The **Asset Catalog**: credited palettes, GB Cartographer's own graphics and eleven CC0 packs from OpenGameArt as
  tilesets, stamps, auto-tiles, sprites and scenes, each piece naming its maker.
- A **demo project** to try it on, and **Settings** for the font (OpenDyslexic included) and the theme.

### New since the last alpha

- 3D Snapshot; the Asset Catalog with eleven credited CC0 art packs; the demo project (now shipped in the app).
- Palette fill (**J**), Pattern rectangle (**Shift+D**); the canvas grows or shrinks a column or row on any side.
- Settings: font and theme (OLED Game Boy green, dark fuchsia, slate, OLED orange, DMG, Pocket, Berry…).
- One-page wizards in a new look; an intro on the start screen.
- Many fixes from a full review, among them: saving after a resize keeps every tile's palette in place; renames never
  replace another file; sprite sheets only grow right and down; see-through pixels in backgrounds save as the
  lightest shade; the desktop app keeps its session when reopened and opens only web links.
- Checked with GB Studio 4.3.2's own builder: painted pixels, tile palettes, priority, collisions, palettes and slots,
  and a resized background all reach the game. (A tileset's tile palettes don't: GB Studio doesn't use them.)

### Opening it the first time (the builds aren't signed)

- **macOS:** open the `.dmg` and drag GB Cartographer to Applications. The first time you open it, macOS says it
  can't check the app for malware (checking needs a paid Apple developer account). Click **Done**, then go to System
  Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to GB Cartographer, then **Open Anyway**
  again. Or run this once in Terminal: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/GB Cartographer.app"`
- **Windows:** if SmartScreen says it protected your PC, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Linux:** make the AppImage executable (`chmod +x GB-Cartographer-*.AppImage`) and run it.
