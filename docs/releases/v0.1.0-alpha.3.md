The third alpha of **GB Cartographer**, a pixel painter for [GB Studio](https://www.gbstudio.dev) projects.

> **Experimental alpha.** Only use it on GB Studio projects you have backed up (a copy, or version control): it
> writes into the project when you save. Close GB Studio while you work.

### What it does

- Open a GB Studio 4 project (4.2.0 – 4.3.2) and paint its backgrounds, sprite sheets, tilesets, fonts, emotes,
  avatars and UI pictures in their real palettes.
- Paint palettes straight onto tiles, put palettes in a scene's slots, manage the project's palettes.
- Tile tools: linked tiles, a seamless view, a priority brush, saved stamps, a text tool with your font sheets.
- See GB Studio's limits: the tile budget (with the tiles that repeat marked), screen edges, project health.
- Wizards: picture to background, dawn / sunset / night palettes, a palette set from Lospec or a picture, a new
  background, a tile budget fixer, a project check-up, and live cave / dungeon and overworld generators.
- The Map Room: lay backgrounds out as an adventure-style grid of screens and check that their edges meet.
- Graphics: credited palette sets (CC0 and public domain) and 54 placeholder graphics (CC0) to paint over.
- Backups of every file it overwrites, restorable from Backups.

### New in this alpha

- **The Mac app opens.** In 0.1.0-alpha.1 and alpha.2 macOS called the app "damaged" and wouldn't open it: the app
  wasn't signed as a whole. It is now (ad-hoc, without an Apple account), so macOS shows its usual message for apps
  from outside the App Store, and **Open Anyway** works (see below).
- Project health's **Open** on a palette shows it in the palette manager; long palette names show in full on hover;
  the time-of-day button says **Day, night, sunset**.
- Generators draw screen edges in cyan (red was hard to see on red palettes) and count **unique tiles**; Map Room
  labels are easier to read; small layout fixes in windows, the sprite frames and messages.

### Opening it the first time (the builds aren't signed)

- **macOS:** open the `.dmg` and drag GB Cartographer to Applications. The first time you open it, macOS says it
  can't check the app for malware (checking needs a paid Apple developer account). Click **Done**, then go to System
  Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to GB Cartographer, then **Open Anyway**
  again. Or run this once in Terminal: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/GB Cartographer.app"`
- **Windows:** if SmartScreen says it protected your PC, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Linux:** make the AppImage executable (`chmod +x GB-Cartographer-*.AppImage`) and run it.

### Known limits

- No demo project in this build: its art joins once its artists have said yes. Open your own (backed-up) GB Studio
  project instead.
- Tested on macOS; the Windows and Linux builds are new. Please report problems in the issues.
